Our Expertise

Advanced Fumigation & Pest Management

We understand that pests pose serious health risks and property damage, especially in Nigerian climate conditions. Our approach moves beyond simple spraying; we use Integrated Pest Management (IPM) to provide long-term protection.

Our certified technicians conduct detailed site risk assessments, utilizing highly effective and safety-approved chemical treatments to eradicate the toughest infestations, including bed bugs, termites, and rodents. We guarantee safe application and effective results.

Key Offerings

Specialist Treatments

Bed Bug Eradication

We use intensive heat and chemical methods for complete elimination of persistent bed bug infestations.

Mosquito & Flying Pest Control

Targeted treatments to control disease-carrying mosquitoes, flies, and other flying insects.

Termite & Rodent Management

Protection against structural damage caused by termites and disease spread by rats and mice.

Guaranteed Service

Don't share your home or business with unwanted pests.

All Gofumigation services come with a satisfaction guarantee. If pests return within the agreed timeframe, we provide a full re-treatment at no extra cost. We are committed to permanent, lasting results across Lagos and beyond.
