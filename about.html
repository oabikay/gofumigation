Our Story

Creating Safe & Pest-Free Environments Across Nigeria

Gofumigation was founded with a clear mission: to protect Nigerian families and businesses from the health hazards and damage caused by pests. We combine advanced, certified fumigants with locally tailored strategies. Our team is trained to deliver safe, permanent relief from all infestations.

Our Values

Integrity, Safety, and Guaranteed Results

We believe pest control must be safe, transparent, and effective. That means using non-toxic solutions around sensitive areas, offering a clear service guarantee, and always putting the client's health and property first. We operate with honesty and precision in every job.

Certified & Licensed

We provide you the quality with perfect credibility

All our technicians hold the necessary Nigerian health and safety certifications for chemical application and pest management. We stay compliant with all national and local regulations, ensuring every treatment we perform is lawful, safe, and effective.

We specialize in treating the toughest Nigerian pests, including stubborn bed bugs, aggressive mosquitoes, and destructive termites, utilizing state-of-the-art equipment and environmentally responsible methods tailored for the tropical climate.

Simple Steps

Our 3-Step Process

Thorough Inspection

We conduct a detailed, non-invasive inspection to identify the pest type, source, and scope of infestation.

Custom Treatment Plan

A targeted plan is designed, choosing the safest and most powerful chemicals for the specific pest and location.

Guaranteed Eradication

Our certified technicians execute the treatment safely and follow up to ensure the pests are completely eliminated.

Trained Professionals

Meet Our Certified Technicians

Our team consists of highly-trained and licensed fumigation experts who adhere strictly to global safety protocols. We don't just spray chemicals; we provide expert advice and long-term pest prevention strategies to keep your property protected.
